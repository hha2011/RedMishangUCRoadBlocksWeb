Block

Rotation system

The block itself remains a normal cube. The blockstate controls the model's Y rotation.

Diagonal Facing is therefore not a special diagonal-shaped block. It is simply a four-direction property whose model is rotated.

Facing Angles

These values are the Minecraft blockstate y rotations.

0° = no rotation
90° = quarter turn
180° = half turn
270° = three-quarter turn

Diagonal defaults are 45°, 135°, 225° and 315°.
